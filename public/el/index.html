          About Hagioi

          Hagioi is an interactive map of Orthodox Christian saints, showing where each saint was born, lived, or was martyred.

          Use the Feast Days panel to open the monthly feast calendar, filter saints by date range, search by name, or optionally find historical saint-associated locations near you.

          The near-me lookup is opt-in and runs only in your browser; it does not verify present-day shrines, churches, or pilgrimage facilities.
